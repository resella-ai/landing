# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who resell clothing online and need to prepare product listings for marketplaces.

## Product Purpose

Resella helps sellers turn a photo of a garment into curated product imagery and a ready-to-use description. Success means giving sellers materials they can take to the marketplace where they already sell.

## Positioning

Resella prepares curated imagery and a listing description from the same garment photo. Its differentiation from other tools has not yet been established.

## Operating Context

During the beta, sellers download the generated images, copy the description, and publish the listing manually on their chosen marketplace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Resella turns a garment photo into curated images and a listing description.
- The first garment is free, and no payment card is required.
- Direct integrations with Vinted, Depop, and eBay are not currently available.
- The product is in beta.

## Brand Commitments

- The product name is Resella.
- Marketplace marks may be shown as examples of places where sellers can publish. They do not represent customer proof or a direct integration.

## Evidence on Hand

- The project contains Resella brand assets and example garment imagery under `src/assets/`.
- No real testimonials or product-performance data are available to publish. Do not invent customers, results, or benchmarks.

## Product Principles

- Start from the seller's garment photo.
- Prepare imagery and copy the seller can use in a listing.
- Keep marketplace choice and publishing in the seller's hands during the beta.
- Describe beta capabilities and integration limits accurately.
- Make no claims of customer outcomes or performance without real evidence.
